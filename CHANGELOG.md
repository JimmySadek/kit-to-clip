# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

The first numbered release. Kit to Clip can now find, set up and use extra powers when a video needs them, and it
remembers how you like your videos.

**New powers, all free and on your own computer.** Kit to Clip offers them only when a job needs one, and asks before
adding anything:
- **Voice-over:** a natural voice reads your script, in 12 voices and 9 languages.
- **Captions:** your words with exact timings, burned in, with the spoken word highlighted.
- **Cutouts:** people, products or logos without their background.
- **Text behind a person:** one click on someone in a clip, and a title can pass behind them.
- **Smooth slow motion** that invents the missing frames, plus sharper upscaling for small photos.
- **Lottie files** for websites and apps, made from your logo and played back to check them.
- **Logo files** from .ai, .eps or .pdf turned into clean SVG.
- **Original music** from a description, in whole bars so cuts land on the beat.
- **A 3D logo** that turns, lit and rendered with a transparent background.
- **New looks:** springy motion, living gradient backgrounds, confetti and particles, painted and hand-drawn styles,
  and objects that fall and pile up.
- **Exact edits:** holds and speed changes to the frame, plus timeline files an editor can open.

**New format: Explainer (trial).** You write the script, a voice reads it, and every scene starts on its spoken word.
It comes with captions and five illustration styles: clean, cut paper, risograph, sketchbook and isometric.

**It remembers you.** It knows the brands you used before and offers them first, and it remembers what you liked and
didn't like. It never applies a memory silently. The memory stays on your computer: ask "what do you remember?" or
"forget …" at any time.

**Safer by default:**
- **Flash guard:** every video is checked for flashing that can harm viewers (at most 3 flashes a second).
- **Reduced motion:** website loops come with a still version for people who turn motion off, and loops over
  5 seconds get a pause button.
- **Licence watch:** every tool and every part it brings along is checked. Anything that limits commercial use is
  never offered.
- **Update watch:** once a week at most, it checks for new versions and new ways to use each tool. It changes
  nothing without your yes.

**Also new:** the trial formats Brand reel and Teaser loop are offered as "new" in the menu.
