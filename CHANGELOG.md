# Changelog

## v1.2.0 (2026-11-21)

**Kit to Clip now has a Studio: one local page where you make a film step by step, from the first idea to the
finished video.** You click and choose on the page; Claude does the work in chat. The page lives on your own
computer and refreshes by itself when Claude changes something.

**Seven rooms, one film.**
- **Start:** paste a link or some text, drop your files and pick the brand (or none).
- **Story:** pick one of two or three ideas, then edit the beats, the script for the voice and the story parts.
- **Styles:** browse the 86 catalogue styles, paint which style each part uses and how parts join. If you keep your
  own style collection on your computer, each card plays a live preview.
- **Voice:** bring a voice take from the tool you choose (your own recording, a free voice on your computer, or an
  online voice tool, which means your script text goes to that service). Each take is timed word by word on your
  computer, and words that need a look are flagged.
- **Storyboard:** one rough panel per part and per join, drawn in the film's real styles. Comment on any panel and
  only that panel is drawn again.
- **Music:** three music takes per moment, played on the picture so you choose by ear. Your picks are joined into
  one track for the whole film.
- **Finish:** voice, sound effects, music and captions are mixed into the final film, levelled for web playback,
  then exported per platform.

**You stay in charge.**
- The four ✋ stops (script, storyboard, music, final film) are now buttons on the page too. A click counts only
  for the version you saw. If anything it covered changes later, the stop opens again and Claude asks again.
- The final film is not built until your ✋ Music yes is recorded.
- Music never switches to a backup sound without telling you. A temporary practice track is only made when you ask
  for one, and it is clearly marked on screen and in the finished film's notes.
- Music added by hand from outside the Music room is labelled as outside music.
- Screens use plain words.

**Also new:**
- Explainer looks and a style gallery you can browse without setting anything up.
- A written brief for every video, so a film can be paused and picked up again later.
- Brand rules are read in full before any layout is made.

**Good to know:**
- Real music takes need the free music generator on your computer (a one-time download of about 9.4 GB, with your
  yes). The Music and Finish rooms were tested with test tones only, not yet with real generated music on a real
  film.
- Everything still works for free on your own computer. No account or paid service is needed.

## v1.1.0 (2026-10-09)

**Kit to Clip now plans like a motion director, and sounds like it means it.** This release comes from a long test day:
the same film was made five ways and judged by eye and ear, and Kit to Clip now works the way that won.

**Better planning, so videos look designed, not like slides.**
- Before building, Kit to Clip now uses two design skills: one shapes the look (type, colour, layout, a bold idea), the
  other critiques the plan and the stills before you see them.
- For a known kind of video (a social short, a product ad, a launch film, an app demo, an explainer, a highlight edit,
  kinetic type, a data story...) it can also read a specialist's notes for that kind of video, when they fit.
- Its extra powers (living backgrounds, particles, 3D logos, cutouts, hand-drawn looks...) are now part of the plan
  from the start, not an afterthought.
- When you want something new, it can sketch five or six quite different directions quickly, so you pick with your eyes.
- Every scene gets a real layout with readable sizes, so nothing shrinks into an empty corner.

**Music and sound effects that feel real.**
- **Original music by default** from the free music generator on your computer (a one-time download of about 9.4 GB,
  with your yes). Kit to Clip makes a few takes, lines each one up with the beat, fixes endings that fade away, and
  keeps the best one. You can still listen to every take.
- **Recorded sound effects by default:** real impacts, clicks and digital sweeps from free sound packs (no account,
  free for commercial use), added with your yes. The built-in synthesised sounds stay as a backup.
- Every sound comes from something you can see moving, and the big moves land on the beat.

**How it works with you.**
- The agent you talk to plans, builds and checks your video itself. When your setup allows it, it can hand quick,
  simple jobs (like sketching many directions) to faster helpers, but it always stays in charge.
- After delivery, it no longer polishes on its own: if it sees something worth fixing, it tells you what and how long,
  and you decide.

**Also:** your stills stay on your computer (from v1.0.1), a check for safe zones by any platform name, and a new README
with a short film Kit to Clip made about itself.

## v1.0.1 (2026-10-09)

**A privacy fix: your frames now always stay on your computer.** If your computer had a Google Gemini key (or an
OpenRouter or Vertex key) set up for something else, the video engine sent your stills to that online service to
describe them, without asking. The same could happen when reading a brand's website. Kit to Clip now switches those
keys off for every video command and always asks the engine to stay local. Nothing else changes, and you don't need to do
anything.

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

The first numbered release. Kit to Clip can now find, set up and use extra powers when a video needs them, and it
remembers how you like your videos.

**New powers, all free and on your own computer.** Kit to Clip offers them only when a job needs one, and asks before
adding anything:
- **Voice-over:** a natural voice reads your script, in 12 voices and 9 languages.
- **Captions:** your words with exact timings, burned in, with the spoken word highlighted.
- **Cutouts:** people, products or logos without their background.
- **Text behind a person:** one click on someone in a clip, and a title can pass behind them.
- **Smooth slow motion** that invents the missing frames, plus sharper upscaling for small photos.
- **Lottie files** for websites and apps, made from your logo and played back to check them.
- **Logo files** from .ai, .eps or .pdf turned into clean SVG.
- **Original music** from a description, in whole bars so cuts land on the beat.
- **A 3D logo** that turns, lit and rendered with a transparent background.
- **New looks:** springy motion, living gradient backgrounds, confetti and particles, painted and hand-drawn styles,
  and objects that fall and pile up.
- **Exact edits:** holds and speed changes to the frame, plus timeline files an editor can open.

**New format: Explainer (trial).** You write the script, a voice reads it, and every scene starts on its spoken word.
It comes with captions and five illustration styles: clean, cut paper, risograph, sketchbook and isometric.

**It remembers you.** It knows the brands you used before and offers them first, and it remembers what you liked and
didn't like. It never applies a memory silently. The memory stays on your computer: ask "what do you remember?" or
"forget …" at any time.

**Safer by default:**
- **Flash guard:** every video is checked for flashing that can harm viewers (at most 3 flashes a second).
- **Reduced motion:** website loops come with a still version for people who turn motion off, and loops over
  5 seconds get a pause button.
- **Licence watch:** every tool and every part it brings along is checked. Anything that limits commercial use is
  never offered.
- **Update watch:** once a week at most, it checks for new versions and new ways to use each tool. It changes
  nothing without your yes.

**Also new:** the trial formats Brand reel and Teaser loop are offered as "new" in the menu.
